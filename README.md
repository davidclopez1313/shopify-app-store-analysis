# Shopify App Store Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze Shopify apps, customer reviews, ratings, and developer engagement.

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX  
**Reporting Period:** January 2018 – December 2024

## Key Insights

- The dashboard includes **500 apps** and approximately **8,000 reviews**.
- The average app rating is **4.19 out of 5**.
- **Sales and Conversion** has the largest number of apps, followed by SEO.
- Review activity increased significantly from 2022 to 2024.
- Only **24.8% of reviews received a developer reply**, showing an opportunity to improve customer communication.

## Business Impact

The increase in reviews suggests growing engagement with Shopify apps. However, the low developer reply rate creates an opportunity to improve customer support and satisfaction. Competition in Sales and Conversion also makes it important for developers to differentiate their apps.

## Recommendations

- **Improve communication:** Respond more consistently to customer reviews.
- **Stand out from competitors:** Use ratings and feedback to identify ways to improve apps in Sales and Conversion.
- **Monitor trends:** Compare review activity over time using year-over-year, YTD, and MTD metrics.

## Dashboard Pages

- **Overview:** KPIs, app categories, review trends, and filters.
- **Trend Analysis:** Monthly review trends, YTD/MTD metrics, and previous-year comparisons.
