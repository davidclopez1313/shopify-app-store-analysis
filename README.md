# Shopify App Store Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Shopify App Store using Power BI to understand app categories, customer reviews, ratings, and developer engagement.

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX  
**Reporting Period:** January 2018 – December 2024

**Dashboard:** Shopify App Store Analysis

## Key Insights

The dashboard includes **500 apps** and approximately **8,000 reviews**, with an average rating of **4.19 out of 5**. Review activity increased over time, especially from 2022 to 2024.

**Sales and Conversion** has the largest number of apps, while **SEO** has the highest total number of reviews. These findings show that app availability and customer review activity vary across categories. Only **24.8% of reviews received a developer reply**, showing an opportunity to improve communication with users.

## Business Impact

The increase in review activity suggests growing engagement with Shopify apps. Sales and Conversion has a large number of apps competing in the same category, while the high review volume in SEO makes customer feedback in that category especially useful to analyze.

Although the average rating is positive, the low developer reply rate suggests that developers have an opportunity to improve customer communication and respond more consistently to feedback.

## Recommendations

- **Improve developer engagement:** Respond more consistently to customer reviews and use feedback to identify areas for improvement, especially since only 24.8% of reviews received a developer reply.
- **Stand out in Sales and Conversion:** Since this category has the largest number of apps, developers should look for ways to differentiate their products and compete more effectively.
- **Learn from SEO reviews:** Since SEO has the highest total number of reviews, developers should analyze customer feedback to understand user needs and identify opportunities to improve their apps.
- **Monitor review trends:** Compare review activity in 2023 and 2024 to measure year-over-year changes and identify which categories contribute most to review growth.

## Dashboard Pages

- **Overview:** KPIs, app categories, review trends, and filters, including the Year slicer.
- **Trend Analysis:** Review trends over time, year-over-year comparisons, and YTD/MTD metrics.
