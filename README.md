# Shopify App Store Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Shopify App Store using Power BI to understand app categories, customer reviews, ratings, and developer engagement.

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX  
**Reporting Period:** 2018–2024

**Dashboard:** Shopify App Store Analysis

**Reporting Period:** January 2018 – December 2024

### Key Insight
The dashboard includes 500 apps and approximately 8,000 reviews, with an average rating of 4.19 out of 5. Review activity increased significantly over time, especially from 2022 to 2024. Sales and Conversion and SEO have the highest numbers of apps among the categories shown. Only 24.8% of reviews received a developer reply.

### Business Impact
The growth in reviews suggests increasing customer engagement with Shopify apps. Although ratings are generally positive, the low developer reply rate shows an opportunity to improve communication with users.

### Recommendation
I recommend that developers respond more consistently to customer reviews and monitor feedback to identify areas for improvement. This can help improve customer satisfaction and make apps more competitive.

## Dashboard Pages
- **Overview:** KPIs, app categories, review trends, and filters.
- **Trend Analysis:** Review trends over time, year-over-year comparisons, and YTD/MTD metrics.
